# 1. Dreamcast research

Sources reviewed (August 2026): `dreamcast.in`, `dreamcast.co`, `godreamcast.com`, product pages for registration, check-in/badging, mobile app, matchmaking, cashless, WhatsApp, expo management; comparison pages vs Cvent / Bizzabo / Swoogo; case studies (IMC 2025, JLF 2026, SHRM, Startup Mahakumbh, Indus Food Expo, World Food India, Zomaland, Mumbai Tech Week, Big Billion Property Sale); third-party coverage of Fastest Indian, Ticket ka ATM, and Aadhaar/OVSE certification.

Dreamcast's marketing is sales-led. There is no public self-serve signup, no public price list, and no public API docs. Treat feature lists as **claimed capability**, not verified software modules. Several items are hardware, humans, or studio services.

---

## 1.1 Positioning

**Category they claim:** Enterprise event-tech suite for in-person, virtual, and hybrid events.

**Category they actually occupy:** India-first **event execution partner**. Software is the control plane. Hardware and on-site people are the product customers remember.

**Taglines and promises that matter:**

- "We disappear behind your brand" — white-label microsites, forms, apps
- "Your data stays yours" — they sell against marketplace platforms that harvest attendees
- "No commission on your sign-ups" — they sell against Eventbrite-style take rates
- "10-second check-in / 10-second badge" — they sell speed at the gate, not software elegance
- Event-to-event pricing, optional packaged series pricing
- 15+ years, 1000+ event teams, claimed scale from ~500 to 500,000+ attendees

**Target customers**

| Segment | Evidence | Why they buy Dreamcast |
|---------|----------|------------------------|
| Large Indian conferences / associations | SHRM, Mumbai Tech Week | Multi-category registration, badges, on-site staff |
| Trade exhibitions / expos | Indus Food Expo, IMC (1,200+ exhibitors, 250k visitors) | Exhibitor portals, lead retrieval, zone access |
| Government / high-security | World Food India, UIDAI/Aadhaar pitch | Invite-only, identity verification, access control |
| Mega public / cultural | Jaipur Literature Festival (~300k) | High-footfall entry, not a SaaS form |
| Festivals / consumer | Zomaland | Cashless RFID + box office + gate staff |
| Corporate / MICE | Automation Anywhere, WRI | Custom workflows + humans who absorb last-minute change |
| Real estate / lead-gen festivals | Big Billion Property Sale | Registration + fast entry for sales events |

**Event sizes they optimize for:** mid-to-mega. Their own content says they can be "more functionality than needed for very small events." Under ~200 attendees, Dreamcast is commercially and operationally overweight.

**Sales motion:** Book a demo / talk to an expert. Product consultant at the start. Event coordinator for dry runs. 24×7 support. This is **services-wrapped software**, not product-led growth.

**Pricing:** Custom. Not SaaS seats in the Luma/Bizzabo sense. Closer to an event production bid: attendees × modules × hardware × on-site days. Their FAQ states they work event-to-event, with packaged cost for series.

## 1.6 Implications for Eventsliner.live

1. **Copy the data model, not the company.** Registration → attendee → credential → check-in is the spine. Kiosks are not.
2. **WhatsApp is mandatory in India, but it is a channel, not a product.** Build a communication engine. Rent the BSP.
3. **Self-serve is the opening.** Dreamcast cannot cheaply serve a 80-person workshop or a 300-person paid conference that wants to launch tonight.
4. **No commission is a positioning weapon** they already use against Eventbrite. Decide Eventsliner's take-rate story before coding checkout.
5. **Brand disappearance** (white-label) is table stakes for serious organizers. Do not watermark attendee-facing pages with Eventsliner in a way that fights the organizer.
6. **Scale theater is expensive.** Designing day-one for 500,000 concurrent check-ins is how you never ship. Design the *model* for it (idempotent scans, offline queue, credential hashing) and the *deployment* for 2,000.
7. **Aadhaar and facial recognition are enterprise/security products** with legal residual risk. They are Phase 10, if ever, and only through certified vendors.
